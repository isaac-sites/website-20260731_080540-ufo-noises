## Introduction

When several people in different neighbourhoods report the same unusual overhead sound, the timing of those reports can become one of the strongest tests of whether the source was actually moving. Instead of treating each witness account in isolation, comparing when the sound was first heard, when it was loudest and when it faded can reveal a consistent travel path that matches an aircraft or another mobile source. If the reports do not show a coherent progression, they may instead point towards a fixed industrial source whose sound was carried unusually far by atmospheric conditions. Used carefully, cross-neighbourhood timing transforms scattered anecdotes into evidence that can be checked against known flight paths, weather conditions and geography rather than relying on impressions alone.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.faa.gov/noise/aviation_noise/community_response" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: faa.gov">[faa.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">faa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Community Response to Noise &#124; Federal Aviation Administration</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">July 22, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 22, 2022</span></span></span>

## Can neighbouring reports trace a sound's path?

A single witness can easily misjudge where a [low-frequency]({{ 'low-frequency/' | relative_url }}) sound originated. Human hearing is poor at locating deep, sustained tones, especially at night or under cloudy skies. However, a series of independent reports from different locations provides information that no individual observer possesses.

If a sound source is moving, the reports should usually show a logical sequence. For example, residents in one district hear the sound first, people in the next neighbourhood report it a minute or two later, and those farther along the same line hear it last. Even allowing for differences in reaction time and the delay caused by the finite speed of sound, the overall pattern often follows the movement of an aircraft.

By contrast, if people across a wide area report hearing the sound begin and end at nearly the same time, the explanation may be different. A stationary industrial source, large ventilation system or distant motorway can become audible over many kilometres when atmospheric conditions refract sound back towards the ground. Under those conditions, people in several neighbourhoods may all notice the same distant source without it having travelled between them.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.faa.gov/noise/aviation_noise/community_response" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: faa.gov">[faa.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">faa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Community Response to Noise &#124; Federal Aviation Administration</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">July 22, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 22, 2022</span></span></span>

## Building a reliable witness timeline

The value of multiple reports depends more on consistency than on the number of witnesses. A handful of accurately timed observations is often more useful than dozens of vague recollections.

Useful information includes:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* The exact or approximate time the sound was first noticed.
* When it became loudest.
* When it faded or stopped.
* The observer's location.
* Whether the sound appeared to move or remained in one direction.
* Whether any visual observations accompanied the sound.

</div>

Independent accounts are especially valuable because they reduce the risk that later discussion influences people's memories. Reports collected from social media, neighbourhood groups or emergency call logs can sometimes be arranged chronologically to reveal a clear progression across an urban area.

Maps become particularly informative when each report is plotted with its timestamp. Even without sophisticated acoustic modelling, a moving pattern often becomes visually obvious once locations are arranged in time order.

## Patterns that indicate a moving aircraft

A moving aircraft generally produces several recognisable timing characteristics.

**A consistent geographic progression.** Reports advance steadily across neighbouring districts in the same direction rather than appearing randomly.

**Reasonable travel times.** The intervals between reports broadly match the speed of an aircraft after allowing for sound propagation delays. Witnesses rarely experience perfectly sequential timing because sound from a high-altitude aircraft may reach different observers after the aircraft has already moved well beyond its closest point.

**Changing sound character.** Observers ahead of the aircraft's path may describe an approaching rumble, while those behind it report a receding roar. Different neighbourhoods often describe different phases of the same event.

**Agreement with known operations.** When the inferred movement aligns with recorded commercial flights, known helicopter routes or military training areas, the timing evidence becomes substantially stronger. [Aircraft noise]({{ 'aircraft-noise/' | relative_url }}) depends not only on the flight path but also on aircraft type, engine power, altitude and local weather, meaning neighbouring communities can experience noticeably different sound levels from the same flight.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.faa.gov/noise/aviation_noise/community_response" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: faa.gov">[faa.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">faa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Community Response to Noise &#124; Federal Aviation Administration</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">July 22, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 22, 2022</span></span></span>

## Patterns that suggest a fixed source

Some timing patterns point away from an overhead moving object.

A nearly simultaneous onset across multiple neighbourhoods is difficult to reconcile with a single aircraft passing overhead unless the witnesses are very close together. Instead, it may indicate that changing atmospheric conditions suddenly made a distant fixed source audible over a much larger area.

Other indicators include:

* Reports remaining strongest from the same compass direction regardless of observer location.
* Similar descriptions continuing for long periods without a clear progression.
* Multiple evenings showing the same timing under comparable weather conditions.
* Correlation with industrial operating schedules rather than aircraft movements.

Temperature inversions deserve particular attention. During an inversion, warmer air overlies cooler surface air, bending sound waves back towards the ground. This can allow distant machinery, transport corridors or airport operations to be heard across neighbourhoods that would normally be outside the audible range, creating the impression of an [unexplained]({{ 'unexplained/' | relative_url }}) overhead source.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://prod-01-alb-www-noaa.woc.noaa.gov/jetstream/lightning/sound-of-thunder" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: prod-01-alb-www-noaa.woc.noaa.gov">[NOAA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">prod-01-alb-www-noaa.woc.noaa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Sound of Thunder &#124; National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Sound of Thunder &#124; National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration...</span></span></span>

## Interpreting disagreements between witnesses

Conflicting reports do not necessarily invalidate the evidence. Several factors naturally produce variation:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* People's clocks may differ by a minute or more.
* Many witnesses estimate times after the event rather than recording them immediately.
* Buildings, hills and vegetation alter local sound levels.
* Low-frequency sounds are difficult for humans to localise accurately.
* Wind and atmospheric layering can cause one neighbourhood to hear the sound much more clearly than another.

</div>

Rather than expecting perfect agreement, investigators should look for the overall trend. If independent reports consistently form a directional sequence, that pattern is often more informative than minor disagreements over exact times.

## Why timing evidence matters

Cross-neighbourhood timing is one of the simplest ways to distinguish between an apparently mysterious overhead sound and an ordinary moving source. It does not identify the source by itself, but it provides a testable hypothesis that can then be compared with aircraft tracking data, weather records and local activity.

Within the broader task of identifying ordinary explanations for reported [UFO noises]({{ 'ufo-noises/' | relative_url }}), this approach shifts the discussion from isolated impressions to measurable patterns. A sound that appears to "travel" through successive neighbourhoods is far more consistent with a moving aircraft or similar vehicle than with a stationary mystery, while reports that remain synchronised across a wide area may instead reflect unusual sound propagation from a fixed source rather than anything overhead.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.faa.gov/noise/aviation_noise/community_response" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: faa.gov">[faa.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">faa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Community Response to Noise &#124; Federal Aviation Administration</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">July 22, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 22, 2022</span></span></span>

    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
