## Introduction

One of the most common conventional explanations for apparently silent, hovering UFOs is surprisingly simple: the object is an ordinary aircraft flying almost directly towards or away from the observer. When an aircraft's motion is aligned with the viewer's line of sight, it produces very little sideways movement across the sky. Combined with bright landing lights and uncertain distance [perception]({{ 'perception/' | relative_url }}), this geometry can create a powerful illusion that the aircraft is stationary, hovering, or moving in impossible ways. If the aircraft is still several kilometres away, its engine noise may be faint, delayed or masked by the surrounding environment, reinforcing the impression of an eerily silent object. This combination of perspective, lighting and acoustics has been recognised both in aviation safety literature and in investigations of mistaken aerial sightings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://skybrary.aero/index.php/articles/visual-illusions" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skybrary.aero">[Skybrary]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skybrary.aero</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Visual Illusions &#124; SKYbrary Aviation Safety</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visual Illusions &#124; SKYbrary Aviation Safety...</span></span></span>

## Why Head-On Motion Is Hard to Judge

Human vision is far better at detecting movement across the field of view than movement directly towards or away from the observer. A car driving across a road is obviously moving because its position changes rapidly against the background. A car driving straight towards you, however, changes very little in apparent position until it becomes quite close.

The same principle applies to aircraft. An airliner approaching on a landing path may travel at well over 200 km/h while appearing almost motionless because its angular position in the sky changes only slowly. Instead of sweeping sideways, it simply grows gradually larger.

## How Landing Lights Reinforce the Hovering Effect

Landing lights are designed to be extremely bright and highly focused so that aircraft are visible over long distances during approach and departure.

Viewed nearly head-on, these lights can dominate everything else:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* the fuselage becomes difficult to distinguish;
* navigation lights may be overwhelmed by the white beam;
* the aircraft's outline disappears into darkness;
* the observer effectively sees only one or two brilliant points of light.

</div>

This creates a misleading impression. Because the illuminated point changes position only slowly, the light appears suspended in space even though the aircraft is steadily closing the distance.

At night this effect becomes stronger because the observer loses most of the depth cues available during daylight. A single bright light floating against a dark sky provides little information about its true distance or speed.

A further complication is that brighter objects are often perceived as being closer than they really are. If a witness subconsciously assumes the aircraft is nearby, the absence of loud engine noise may seem extraordinary even though the aircraft is actually several kilometres away. Aviation guidance recognises that bright lighting and sparse visual references can distort judgement of distance and position during night operations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.faa.gov/Air_traffic/publications/atpubs/aim_html/chap8_section_1.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: faa.gov">[Federal Aviation Administration]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">faa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Federal Aviation Administration Chapter 8. ## Why the Aircraft May Seem Silent

The apparent silence is usually not because the aircraft is unusually quiet, but because the observer has overestimated how close it is.

Several ordinary factors contribute:

* **Distance.** Modern aircraft become much quieter at several kilometres' range than many people expect.
* **[Sound delay]({{ 'sound-delay/' | relative_url }}).** Light reaches the observer essentially instantaneously, while [sound travels]({{ 'sound-travel/' | relative_url }}) at roughly 343 metres per second under typical conditions. An approaching aircraft may therefore be seen well before noticeable engine noise arrives.
* **Wind and atmosphere.** Wind direction, temperature layers and background environmental noise can weaken or redirect aircraft sound.
* **Expectation.** If the object is mistakenly believed to be only a few hundred metres away, the lack of obvious engine noise appears mysterious even though it is entirely consistent with a much greater distance.

These acoustic factors complement the visual illusion rather than creating it independently.

## Clues That Reveal an Approaching or Departing Aircraft

Although the hovering impression can be convincing, there are usually subtle clues that distinguish a conventional aircraft from an unidentified object.

Useful indicators include:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Gradual brightening.** The landing lights steadily increase in intensity as the aircraft approaches.
* **Increasing apparent size.** Even if sideways movement is minimal, the lights slowly separate and the aircraft eventually becomes visible.
* **Navigation light pattern.** As the viewing angle changes, the standard red, green and white aviation lights become easier to identify.
* **Predictable track.** The object continues along a straight approach or departure corridor rather than making abrupt manoeuvres.
* **Eventual engine noise.** As the aircraft draws nearer or passes abeam, engine sound often becomes noticeable and aligns with the visual observation.

</div>

Once the aircraft begins to pass the observer rather than remain nearly head-on, its sideways motion becomes much more obvious and the hovering illusion rapidly disappears.

## Night-Time Perception Can Add Further Illusions

A head-on aircraft can be mistaken not only for a hovering object but also for one that changes direction unexpectedly.

One contributing factor is **autokinesis**, a well-known visual illusion in which a small light against a dark, featureless background appears to drift if stared at for several seconds. Aviation authorities teach pilots to avoid prolonged fixation on isolated lights because this effect can create false impressions of movement.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://skybrary.aero/index.php/articles/autokinetic-effect" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skybrary.aero">[Skybrary]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skybrary.aero</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Autokinetic Effect &#124; SKYbrary Aviation Safety</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Autokinetic Effect &#124; SKYbrary Aviation Safety...</span></span></span>

This illusion does not mean the aircraft is actually manoeuvring. Instead, the observer's visual system lacks stable reference points and begins interpreting tiny involuntary eye movements as movement of the light itself.

    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
